1. Blend mechanism

Every game-level probability evmax prices comes from one function: EnsembleModelAgent in evmax/agents/models/ensemble_agent.py. The blend combines two sides: a model side (the sector's statistical agents) and a sharp side (the devigged Pinnacle line). The sharp side is the anchor; the model side is the deviation-generating voice.

Execution order

  1. Each model agent predicts independently. An agent returns (true_prob_a, true_prob_b, true_prob_draw, confidence, sample_size, notes) or None when it has no basis to predict (missing state, wrong sector, staleness guard).
  2. Confidence gate. A prediction with confidence ≤ 0.45 is excluded from the blend and recorded in the why-not diagnostics as gated (ensemble_agent.py:598). The gate value is 0.45 for every sector and every model.
  3. Effective weight. Each surviving prediction gets eff_w = w × confidence, where w is the sector's entry in SECTOR_WEIGHT_OVERRIDES (falling back to the model's class weight when the sector has no override). A model whose effective weight is ≤ 0 also drops out — and drops out of model_sources, which matters for the tennis gate (see §4).
  4. Model-side weighted average. Probabilities are averaged with eff_w weights and renormalized by the sum of effective weights. Weights therefore do not need to sum to 1 (soccer's sum is 0.90; NHL's is 0.45 — renormalization makes only the ratios matter).
  5. Model-side isotonic calibration. ModelCalibrator applies a per-sector isotonic correction keyed "{sector}_ensemble" before sharp blending. Sharp lines are already well calibrated; calibrating after the sharp mix would double-correct (ensemble_agent.py:654). Sectors without a fitted calibration get the identity map.
  6. Sharp blend. The final probability is a convex combination:
blended = sw · sharp_devigged + (1 − sw) · calibrated_model_avg where sw = disagreement_ramp( base_sharp_weight, |model − sharp| gap )
  1. Disagreement ramp. When the model side visibly disagrees with sharp, sw ramps up toward a cap — the blend defers to the side that walk-forwards show is usually right (§3).
  2. Adjustment layers. Injury (−10% per-team cap), late-news, rest and playoff adjustments apply on top of the blend. These append tokens to model_sources but are not counted as model signal by the gates (§4).
  3. Diagnostics. Every event carries a fired / gated / missing classification per configured model (BlendedPrediction.diagnostics), persisted to ev_predictions.model_diagnostics and inspectable via evmax cleanup shadow show --why.

Degenerate cases: with no models the blend passes sharp through unchanged (this is what the sharp-passthrough detector hunts for); with no sharp the calibrated model average is used alone.

2. Sharp-weight resolution & auto-tune

The base sharp weight is resolved with three levels of precedence (highest wins):

  1. Per-event override — soccer only: sharp_weight_for_ticker() maps the Kalshi series prefix to a league tier (data/soccer_league_tiers.yaml): top-5 European leagues + UCL/UEL = 0.85, MLS and unmatched series = 0.40.
  2. Per-sector config — sharp_weight_by_sector in data/model_config.json. Current values: nba 0.70, tennis 0.85, baseball 0.88, soccer 0.88 (superseded per-event by the tier map), ufc 0.88, lol 1.00, cs2 1.00.
  3. Global default — sharp_weight = 0.85. Sectors with no per-sector entry (nfl, ncaab, ncaaw, ncaaf, worldcup, wnba, nhl) use this.

evmax cleanup adjust auto-tunes the global weight weekly from the Brier comparison of blended vs sharp probabilities on resolved outcomes, bounded to [0.40, 0.95]. It has never needed to move off 0.85:

Design principle: the edge evmax harvests is mostly Kalshi-vs-Pinnacle price deviation, not out-forecasting Pinnacle. High sharp weights (0.70–1.00) are deliberate: the model side exists to (a) catch the cases where the sharp snapshot is stale, (b) regularize, and (c) prove independent signal exists before a sector is allowed to bet live (the anti-passthrough gates in §4).

3. Disagreement ramp

_disagreement_sharp_weight() boosts the sharp weight when the model side and sharp pull apart. Mechanism: compute the max per-leg |model − sharp| gap; below threshold the base weight is unchanged; above it, linearly ramp toward cap, saturating at saturate_at.

4. Live-play gates

Two structural gates in evmax/agents/odds/ev_gap_agent.py prevent a gap from becoming a live (bankroll-sized) play when the blend's output is not genuinely model-driven. A gap that fails either gate gets full_blend=False: hidden from the play table, Kelly zeroed, excluded from the exposure budget, and demoted to mode='shadow' at persistence — still logged for calibration.

4.1 REQUIRED_BLEND_MODELS — all-of gate (tennis)

A tennis live play requires all four primary models in model_sources: tennis_surface, tennis_serve_return, tennis_form, tennis_advanced (h2h and ranking_trend are optional — they structurally cannot fire on most matches). Rationale: walk-forward (n=2576, 2025+2026) shows the full blend beats sharp by +2.6 Brier/1000 while sparse blends are a wash — a sparse-blend "edge" is thin Kalshi-vs-Pinnacle arb, not model edge.

Gotcha (load-bearing): _blend skips any model with eff_w ≤ 0, so a model set to weight 0 in SECTOR_WEIGHT_OVERRIDES vanishes from model_sources — for a sector with a REQUIRED_BLEND_MODELS entry that silently demotes every play to shadow. Never zero a required blend model's weight; floor it. This is why tennis serve_return sits at 0.10 even though its metric optimum is 0.

4.2 MIN_NONSHARP_MODELS — any-N-of floor

Complementary floor: a live play needs at least min_count genuine model tokens for the listed market types. Tokens in _NON_MODEL_TOKENS count zero: sharp, sharp(capped), injury, late_news, rest, playoff, advance_derived, spread_dist, total_dist, no_side.

Sectors absent from both gates (ufc, lol, cs2, nhl…) are unaffected: sharp dominance there is by design. Soccer totals are deliberately excluded from the floor — no model prices them, so a floor would demote 100% of them.

4.3 The sharp-passthrough detector

The promotion board computes blend divergence — mean |blended − sharp| in probability points — per (sector, market, venue). A moneyline divergence under 0.5 pp means the blend is a sharp passthrough and any apparent EV is venue divergence, not model edge. Reference snapshot (30d ML, 2026-07-19): wnba 4.82 pp vs tennis 0.17 / baseball 0.10 / soccer 0.00.

5. Sector-by-sector blends

Each card shows the sector's blend composition bar: the gray segment is the sharp anchor at the sector's base sharp weight; colored segments are each model's nominal share of the final blend ((1 − sw) × weight / Σweights). Runtime shares differ: each model's weight is multiplied by its live confidence and renormalized, gated models drop out entirely, and the disagreement ramp can push the sharp share higher. Weights come from SECTOR_WEIGHT_OVERRIDES (ensemble_agent.py:85); a weight of 0 in that table means "explicitly excluded" and is listed under the bar, not drawn.

Model weights sum to 0.90 and are renormalized at blend time (shares shown normalized). For MLS (sharp weight 0.40) the model side carries 60% of the blend: poisson 26.7%, xg 16.7%, elo 10.0%, form 6.7%.

  • League tiers are the core design. Walk-forward on 2,606 games: in top-5 European leagues Pinnacle's closing Brier is the informational ceiling (~0.1981–0.2097) and the stat blend at sharp weight 0.40 drags it by 0.003–0.007. Top-tier keeps 15% model contribution as a regularizer; the disagreement ramp (§3) pushes to pure sharp on wide gaps. MLS is thinner and less adversarial, so the models keep the 0.40 default.
  • Poisson is football-only by construction (SUPPORTED_SECTORS = {"soccer", "worldcup"}): Dixon-Coles with draw mass, essential for the 3-way TIE market. The agent-level gate exists because per-sector weight overrides only re-weight listed models — an unlisted poisson would fall back to its 0.30 class weight (this silently contaminated NCAAB and baseball blends before 2026-06-01).
  • xG reads ESPN shots / shots-on-target into soccer_xg_state.json; seed names are canonicalized via NameNormalizer (raw ESPN displayNames orphaned the entire xG seed at predict time until fixed).
  • The MIN_NONSHARP floor (§4.2) exists because of this sector: unseeded MLS produced 11 live sharp-passthrough rows in July 2026.
  • Soccer totals have no model pricing them and are excluded from the floor by product decision.

8. Cross-cutting freshness & contamination guards

Guards that keep stale or contaminated state out of the blend, regardless of sector:

  • Elo/Form staleness (60 days). Both agents return None when their state (or the most recent team record) is >60 days older than the game's event date. Added after frozen March baseball ratings fired at 0.25 weight into June blends. Legacy Elo states without a last_updated stamp are never gated.
  • Seasonal staleness guards. WNBA/NFL efficiency, NCAAB/NCAAW/NCAAF efficiency, and the college sims silence prior-season seeds in-season until the current-season re-seed lands. The WNBA guard exists because the offseason regression script only touches Elo — stale efficiency ratings produced a +24 pp chalk bias over the 2026 opening month before the guard.
  • Preseason contamination guard (2026-08-01). ESPN season.type == 1 games are dropped before the model-update feed — preseason/exhibition results must never train ratings. Fail-open (missing field = regular season); soccer-like sectors exempt (ESPN uses the field differently).
  • Exhibition filter. The WNBA seed's REAL_WNBA_TEAMS allow-list keeps the All-Star Game and international exhibitions out of league averages.
  • Off-season shadow downgrade. get_mode downgrades a YAML-live windowed sector to shadow outside its season_window, so a forced out-of-season scan can never stake bankroll on frozen-seed predictions. Self-restores when the window opens.
  • Double-feed ledger. applied_model_games guarantees each game trains Elo/Form/Poisson/xG state exactly once even when both cleanup resolve and update scores run on the same date (games were measurably double-counted before 2026-07-25).
  • Model-version contamination filters. Shadow metrics exclude rows from superseded code by default (the pitcher→pitcher_v2 rename, the pre-guard sharp-only soccer rows, the June-2026 baseball multi-code-state window). model_version stamps the git SHA.

9. Validation & promotion statistics

The standard lifecycle

  1. Offline walk-forward backtest — parameters swept on a train season, reported on a held-out season, never tuned on holdout. This validates the model.
  2. Shadow mode — full pipeline runs, rows persist with mode='shadow' and captured_yes_price, Kelly zeroed. This validates the live wiring (matching, devig, resolution, injury adjustments), not model edge.
  3. Promotion — evmax cleanup shadow promote <sector>, which refuses below 30 clean current-code resolved rows. The working gate is n≥30 resolved with CLV ≥ 0 on the venue's own book, with adequate close-capture coverage.

Instruments

Key empirical facts the design leans on

  • Entry timing dominates model quality for CLV. Only the <1h-pre-tip entry window carries positive placed-bet CLV (+1.55 pp); the night-before edge is usually a stale Kalshi line reverting to the sharp close. The scan is a watchlist; pick re-fetches live asks.
  • Sector timing verdicts differ. WNBA spread: anchored near-listing entry (lay side) is the validated product. Tennis: near-tip entry is worse than scan-time (+1.34 pp actual vs +0.47 pp rescan, n=121) — the anchored-entry machinery must not be rebuilt for tennis. Baseball totals: flat near tip, no CLV path at all.
  • First value audit (2026-06-27), verified by four independent agents: all sector blends healthy or within noise; zero model-actionable gaps. NBA negative CLV = timing; the soccer +9.21 pp bucket = artifact; the WNBA deficit = already-fixed May contamination.

11. Change-management rules

  1. Sweep on train, report on holdout. Every weight in SECTOR_WEIGHT_OVERRIDES traces to a script under scripts/backtest_* / scripts/sweep_* with an explicit train/holdout split. A weight change without a holdout number does not ship.
  2. Never zero a required blend model. Floor it (tennis serve_return = 0.10). A zero weight removes the model from model_sources and silently shadow-demotes every play in a REQUIRED_BLEND_MODELS sector.
  3. Weight overrides only re-weight listed models. An unlisted model falls back to its class weight — gate unsupported sectors at the agent (poisson's SUPPORTED_SECTORS), not by omission.
  4. New model checklist: add the canonical name to evmax/categories.py::KNOWN_MODELS and the category's models: list in data/categories.yaml — validate_registry() fails at import time if either is missed. Tests are required for any module with real logic.
  5. Parallel stacks stay parallel. NBA / WNBA / college (NCAAB+NCAAW) / NCAAF are four independent efficiency stacks. Shared math cores are allowed only where data source and formulas are identical (the college pair); constants and state files never merge.
  6. CLV is a tripwire, not an objective. Weight fine-tuning targets holdout Brier vs the entry anchor; close-anchored signals sit below the noise floor (tennis §5). Gating plays is never the fix for a model gap — fix the models (reweight / recalibrate) or lean sharper.
  7. Never game the metric. Optimization loops must not touch the eval, fixtures, or holdout; kept iterations pass through the iteration-reviewer gate.
